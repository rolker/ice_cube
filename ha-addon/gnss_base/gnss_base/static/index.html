Our antenna against the references
—






Each reference station's RTK solution of our antenna, turned into our frame: the session mean (fixed epochs, station frame minus ours) minus the expected offset computed from the declared frames (each entry's frame, base_frame) at our epoch, never guessed from the stream. A consistent reference sits at 0, 0, 0: green within 5 cm horizontal and 10 cm up, amber within 20 cm, red beyond or after 30 min without a fix. A reference whose station frame is unknown is shown raw (station frame minus ours, about a metre between NAD83(2011) and ITRF2020), with the reason, and is not in the spread. The spread is max − min per axis over the current references (streaming, a fix in the last 30 min, the expected station): green within 3 cm horizontal and 6 cm up, amber within 10 cm. A fixed solution can still be a false fix: trust a residual that holds for hours, not minutes. Static mode smears an antenna move into a slow drift until the daily reset; the Measured position card catches a move within seconds.
