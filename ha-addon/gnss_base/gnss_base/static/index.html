Measured position (rtkrcv)

—

Single point positioning: expect 1-2 m scatter, and Up several metres high (RTKLIB's default ionosphere model). A steady offset is normal; look for jumps and trends.



Reference check 
—


RTK against a CORS station, static: the session mean converges over minutes to hours. Offsets are the solution minus our RTCM 1005 position (the CORS frame minus ours); the residual is the mean minus the expected offset: green within 5 cm horizontal and 10 cm up, amber within 20 cm, red beyond or after 30 min without a fix. A fixed solution can still be a false fix: trust a residual that holds for hours, not minutes. Static mode smears an antenna move into a slow drift until the daily reset; the Measured position card catches a move within seconds.
