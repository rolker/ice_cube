How far to trust each survey

  • Nov 4 (home base): nearby points agree to 3–4 cm at any time gap, and the deck corner repeated to about 1.5 cm over an hour.
  • Oct 26 (MassDOT): nearby points agree to 1–3 cm within 3 minutes but only 7–8 cm after 25 minutes or more. Oct 18 has too few repeats to tell.
  • Oct 26 static test at the deck corner: five MassDOT mount points read from 16.56 to 16.74 m, an 18 cm spread. Masa alone moved 4 cm in 15 minutes. They agreed within about 7 cm horizontally, so use the home base for heights.
  • Feb 14 and Mar 2 (MassDOT): same pattern by shot order, about 1 cm rising to 4–5 cm. The Feb deck corner read 7.7 cm higher at the end than at the start.
  • The 12.5 cm mean accretion is uncertain by a similar amount, because the deck corner read 4–12 cm higher in February than in January. The residual southeast slope, about 6 cm across the rink, is also within the wander.

Base setups and open items

  • Home base after PPP and MassDOT differ by 1.3 m sideways and 1.2 m up at the deck corner. ITRF2014 to NAD83(2011) predicts nearly the same, so this looks like a datum difference. Stake out in the frame your receiver is using.
  • Oct ground reads about 10 cm above Nov ground. The likely cause is the MassDOT mount point. In the Oct 26 static test the near and masa mounts read about 16.68 m at the deck corner, 10 cm above the 16.58 m that PPP plus the published datum shift gives. Last year's ice thickness depends on this: about 6–13 cm on the Oct level, 14–22 cm on the Nov level.
  • Not in the data: tree and house shade, roof snow, buried lines or septic, and whether the January boat shed outline still stands.
